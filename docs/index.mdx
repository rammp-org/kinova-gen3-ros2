# Kinova Gen3 ROS 2

The ROS 2 interface to a Kinova Gen3 7-DOF arm.

This module is the ROS 2 shell around
[`kinova-gen3-driver`](https://github.com/rammp-org/kinova-gen3-driver), which owns the
1 kHz real-time control loop. This layer translates ROS messages to and from that
driver's plain value types, and **no ROS 2 header ever reaches the real-time thread**.

## What it gives you

Four control tiers, in increasing order of how directly you drive the arm:

- **Planned moves** — `go_to_ee_pose`, `go_to_joint_config`, `go_to_preset`. Give it a
  destination; [rammp-curobo](https://github.com/rammp-org/RAMMP-CuRobo) plans a
  collision-free trajectory and this module executes it.
- **Trajectory execution** — `execute_joint_trajectory`. You supply the waypoints;
  path and goal tolerance guards abort on divergence.
- **Setpoint streaming** — seven controllers over `/setpoint/*` at rate, for policies
  and teleoperation.
- **Arbitration** — who is allowed to command the arm at all, plus a broadcast e-stop
  any node may publish.

## Two things that surprise people

**This module is two containers.** The `go_to_*` actions are clients of the planner's
actions. Run the driver alone and three of the four action servers accept goals that
can never succeed. `deploy/compose.yaml` declares both.

**Velocity mode is not a hold.** Zero velocity means "stop driving", not "stay put" —
and on this hardware the shoulder creeps under gravity at a zero command
([#34](https://github.com/rammp-org/kinova-gen3-driver/issues/34)). Use
`ee_pose_position` to hold a pose.

Something looks broken? **[Troubleshooting](troubleshooting)** collects the
failures people actually hit — starting with the one where `ros2 topic echo` prints
nothing and the driver is fine.
